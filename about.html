About the Founder

Rooted in Community. Driven by Strategy.

Savannah Sumners

Sierra Business Collective was founded on the belief that our lives and professions are meaningful. This vision is worth pursuing with focused intentionality.

Originally from Alabama, I've always been drawn to hard work, community, and a life filled with purpose and adventure. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Healthcare Management from the University of Alabama at Birmingham and later completed my Master of Business Administration from the University of North Alabama.

In 2022, my husband, our two daughters, and I relocated to Carson Valley, drawn by the opportunity to build a life surrounded by mountains, open space, and adventure. When we're not at the ranch, you'll likely find us camping, paddleboarding, hiking, rock climbing, skiing, or simply enjoying the outdoors together.

Horses have been part of my story for as long as I can remember. I spent my childhood summers riding on my aunt's farm in Alabama, and although life eventually took me away from horses for several decades, my daughters' love of riding brought me back. Today, we share our lives with Smokey, our beautiful bay Appendix gelding. Returning to horses alongside my daughters has been a wonderful full-circle experience.

That journey eventually led me to Between Horses & Humans, where I joined the Board of Directors in 2025 and serve as Chair of the Fundraising Committee. Supporting the organization's mission and helping bring the healing and transformative power of horses to more people in our community is something I'm deeply honored to be part of.

Whether I'm helping a business become more organized and efficient or helping an individual take control of an overwhelming list of responsibilities, I bring the same philosophy to my work: listen carefully, think strategically, handle the details, and be someone my clients can trust.
